Privacy Policy

Glide Remote · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Glide Remote does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal data. The app talks only to your TV on your own home network.

What the app does with your network

The app searches your local Wi-Fi network for compatible smart TVs and sends remote-control commands (buttons, pointer movement, typed text, volume, app launches) directly to the TV you choose. It can also request a still picture of the TV screen from the TV to show you a live view. These pictures go only from your TV to your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere else.

Data stored on your device

The following is kept only on your iPhone, using the standard app storage, and is never transmitted to us:

  • The address of your TV and the pairing key the TV gives the app.
  • The TV name you choose, your app shortcuts, and your custom button layouts and game-pad settings.
  • Whether Glide Remote Pro is unlocked (see below).

Deleting the app removes all of this.

In-App Purchase (Glide Remote Pro)

Glide Remote is free to download. Glide Remote Pro is an optional one-time In-App Purchase that unlocks game mode, Remote Cast and custom layouts. It is not a subscription.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your card details, your Apple ID or password, or your name, email or billing address. Glide Remote has no account system and no server of its own, so no purchase information is sent to us.

To know whether Pro is unlocked, the app asks Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone for your purchase record. The app keeps only a simple “Pro unlocked” setting on your iPhone, so Pro also works without an internet connection. That setting is deleted if you delete the app. You can get Pro back at any time, at no cost, with Restore Purchases in Settings › Glide Remote Pro or on the purchase screen, using the same Apple ID.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

The app has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software that collects data. We operate no servers for the app.

Permissions

  • Local Network: required to find and control your TV on your Wi-Fi.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
