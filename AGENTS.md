# Glide Remote support site

This repository is the public support and privacy website for the Glide Remote app. It is not the app itself.

The live site is GitHub Pages from the `main` branch:

https://amrkz.github.io/glide-remote/

Do not push to `main`, and do not merge into `main`, unless Dr Amr asks. A merge publishes the site.

## Rules

- Keep the app name as Glide Remote and Glide Remote Pro.
- Do not add or change a price. These pages do not state a number.
- The public contact email is amroqazzaz@gmail.com. Do not change it. Keep the GitHub issues link as well.
- Do not change what the privacy policy says about data. If a sentence looks wrong, flag it and leave the wording. Known flags are listed below.
- Pages are plain HTML plus `styles.css`. No build step, no tracking, and no third-party fonts or scripts.
- Every page needs a visible email address, a link to the other page, and text that stays readable in light mode, dark mode, and on a phone.

## Policy flags (do not rewrite these)

- The network section calls a TV screen capture both a "still picture" and a "live view". Those are not the same thing. Left as written.
- The policy says data stays on "your iPhone". This repo does not contain the app, so iPad support was not confirmed.
- The policy says screen pictures are never saved or sent anywhere else. That was not checked against the app code, because the app is not in this repo.
- The policy says "we" and "us" without a legal name. Other sites from the same developer use different public names, so this site does not add one.
