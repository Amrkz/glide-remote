Getting started: pair your TV

  1. Turn the TV on. Connect the phone and the TV to the same Wi‑Fi network.
  2. Open Glide Remote TV. On iPhone and iPad, tap Allow when asked to find devices on your local network.
  3. Tap your TV in the list. If it isn't listed, tap Add TV by IP (on the TV: Settings → Network & Internet → your network, or Settings → About → Status).
  4. The TV shows a 6‑character code. Type it in the app and tap Pair. You only do this once per TV.

No TV nearby? Tap Try demo (no TV needed) on the first screen to see every screen without a TV.

FAQ

Which TVs work?

Devices running Android TV or Google TV that include the system's remote service, for example Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, NVIDIA SHIELD and Android TV / Google TV televisions and boxes. LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, Roku and Apple TV are not supported.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign‑in. The remote is free. Optional Glide Remote TV Pro is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) sold by Apple or Google. It adds Game mode and pairing more than one TV. The price in the app always comes from the store. You can restore a purchase in Settings → Unlock Pro → Restore Purchases.

Why does the app ask for local network access? (iPhone / iPad)

The app talks directly to your TV over your home Wi-Fi: it looks for TVs that announce the remote service, and if none appear it checks other addresses on your local network. Then it sends your key presses to the TV you picked. Nothing is sent to the internet. If you tapped “Don't Allow”, the app shows a message with an Open Settings button. You can also open the iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn on Glide Remote TV, then reopen the app.

What can I do with it?

  • Touchpad (swipe to move, tap for OK, hold for a long press), D‑pad, Back, Home, volume, mute, channel and media keys.
  • Search key: opens the TV's own search / Assistant screen. (The phone doesn't record or send voice.)
  • Keyboard: type into the TV's search boxes in 7 languages, or use your phone's keyboard; number pad.
  • Game mode (Pro): gamepad, racing and swipe layouts that press remote keys (games only see remote‑control keys).
  • Several TVs (Pro; your first TV is free): switch between paired TVs, rename them, pick an icon.
  • App shortcuts: open apps on the TV; you can add your own app by its package name.

Troubleshooting

My TV isn't found

  • Check both devices are on the same Wi‑Fi (not a guest network, and not one on 2.4 GHz and the other on a separate “5G” network that is isolated).
  • iPhone / iPad: make sure Local Network is on for the app (see above).
  • Some routers block device discovery (“AP isolation” / “client isolation”). Use Add TV by IP instead.
  • VPN apps on the phone can block local connections: turn the VPN off and try again.

The code is rejected

Codes are 6 characters (0–9 and A–F). Type exactly what the TV shows; a new code appears every time you start pairing. If it keeps failing, close the code screen on the TV and pair again.

It says “Pair again”

The TV no longer remembers this phone (for example after a factory reset or after removing the remote in the TV's settings). Tap Pair again and type the new code.

It keeps connecting / the TV went to sleep

The app reconnects by itself when you come back to it, when the Wi-Fi changes, or when the connection drops. If the TV changed its IP address, the app finds it again by name. If the TV is in standby, the default setting sends Power after connecting (you can turn this off in Settings). You can also save a MAC address in Manage TVs to send a wake packet first — this is hit-or-miss; many Google TV boxes will not wake this way. If the TV is fully off (not standby) and does not wake, turn it on with its own remote first.

Remove a TV

Settings → Manage TVs → tap the TV → Forget. To also remove the phone on the TV side, look in the TV's settings for connected remotes / accessories.

Delete all data

Delete the app. Everything it stores is on your device only (see the privacy policy). To make your TV forget this phone too, remove it in the TV's settings.
