Glide Remote for Android TV

A phone remote for Android TV & Google TV devices.

Help & supportPrivacy policy

Use your iPhone, iPad or Android phone as the remote for your Android TV or Google TV on the same Wi‑Fi: touchpad, D‑pad, media keys, a keyboard for typing on the TV, a game mode, and shortcuts to open apps on the TV.
