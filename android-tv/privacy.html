Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Last updated 24 November 2026 · Applies to Glide Remote for Android TV (“Glide Remote TV” on your home screen) for iPhone, iPad and Android.

In short: Glide Remote TV has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of ours. It doesn't collect your personal data. It talks to the TV you choose on your own local network. If you buy Pro, the payment is handled by Apple or Google — we never see your card or account.

What the app stores, on your device only

  • A remote identity: a security certificate and key the app creates on first launch so your TV can recognise this phone after pairing. Kept in the iPhone Keychain / Android Keystore‑protected storage.
  • Your TVs: for each paired TV, its name, the nickname and icon you chose, its IP address on your network and port numbers, an optional MAC address you type in if you want Wake-on-LAN, and which TV you used last.
  • Your settings: keyboard languages and mode, game‑mode layout and button choices, app shortcuts, apps you added yourself and recently opened apps, and whether to send Power to turn the TV on when you open the app.
  • Pro entitlement: on this device only, whether Pro is unlocked. This is how the app remembers your purchase; it is not sent to us.
  • Home‑screen shortcuts: the names of your paired TVs, shown as quick actions when you long‑press the app icon.

This data never leaves your device, except as described below, and is never sent to us. The Android app opts out of cloud backups. Deleting the app deletes your TVs and settings; on iPhone and iPad, iOS may keep the remote identity in the Keychain after the app is deleted, where only this app can read it.

Network use

  • Finding TVs: the app listens for TVs on your local network that announce the Android TV remote service (Bonjour / mDNS). If none appear after a few seconds, it checks TCP ports 6466 and 6467 on the other addresses of your local subnet (the same /24 as the phone) so a TV that did not announce itself can still be found. You can also type a TV's IP address.
  • Controlling your TV: the app opens an encrypted connection (TLS) directly to your TV on your local network and sends the key presses, text you type in the app's keyboard, and app-launch links you tap. It also checks whether your paired TVs are reachable on the network. If you have typed a MAC address for a TV, the app may send a standard Wake-on-LAN packet to the local broadcast address before connecting; this stays on your local network and often does nothing, because many Google TV devices ignore it.
  • No servers of ours: the app does not contact any server we run. The only times it uses the internet are: when you tap “Help & support”, “Privacy policy” or “Terms of Use”, which open a website in your browser; when you tap “Open Settings” (iPhone), which opens the system Settings app; and when you look up, buy or restore Glide Remote TV Pro, which talks only to Apple (App Store / StoreKit) or Google (Play Billing). We do not receive your payment details, name, or purchase receipts.

What we don't do

  • No sign‑in or account.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos access. The Search key only presses the TV's search button; the app doesn't record audio.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data on our side to sell or share.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
