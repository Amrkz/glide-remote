Glide Remote

A fast touch remote for LG smart TVs running webOS (2014 and later) on your home Wi-Fi.

Works with LG webOS TVs only. Samsung, Sony, Roku, Fire TV and other brands are not supported yet. Glide Remote is an independent app and is not affiliated with LG Electronics.

Contact

Questions or problems? Email amroqazzaz@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, TV model, and a short description of what happened.

You can also open an issue at github.com/Amrkz/glide-remote/issues.

If your TV is not listed

  • Use an LG TV running webOS from 2014 or later. Other brands are not supported.
  • Keep your iPhone and the TV on the same home Wi-Fi. The app talks only to your TV on that network.
  • Allow Local Network access when your iPhone asks for it. The app needs that permission to find and control the TV.

What you can control

The app searches your local Wi-Fi network for compatible smart TVs and sends remote-control commands (buttons, pointer movement, typed text, volume, app launches) directly to the TV you choose. The pairing key the TV gives the app stays on your iPhone.

Glide Remote Pro

Glide Remote is free to download. Glide Remote Pro is an optional one-time In-App Purchase that unlocks game mode, Remote Cast and custom layouts. It is not a subscription.

Payment is handled by Apple. You can get Pro back at any time, at no cost, with Restore Purchases in Settings › Glide Remote Pro or on the purchase screen, using the same Apple ID.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Glide Remote does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal data. The app talks only to your TV on your own home network. There is no account. Deleting the app removes the TV address, pairing key, and other details kept on your iPhone.
